Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This policy covers the gta6-shorts tool (home page).

Who uses it

gta6-shorts is used only by its owner, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. No other people sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission: uploading videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channels, and reading the basic details of those channels (channel name and id) to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • It does not read viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts or payment details.

What it stores

  • The sign-in keys (OAuth tokens) for the owner's channels, kept on the owner's own server with access limited to the owner.
  • A log of its own uploads (video id, title, time).

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The data is used only to upload the owner's videos.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in the Google Account security settings (Third-party apps with account access). The stored keys are then deleted from the server.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the About page of the owner's YouTube channels.
