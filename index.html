gta6-shorts

gta6-shorts is a private publishing tool for two YouTube channels, Wasted Moments and Busted Moments. It turns short gameplay moments into vertical YouTube Shorts and uploads them to those channels through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is used only by the owner of those channels. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
